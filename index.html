Ancient Greece · Demeter and Persephone

Understand the Mysteries of Eleusis.

Eleusis was a sacred place near Athens in ancient Greece. There, the Mysteries honored Demeter and Persephone through a tradition of initiation. Their story tells of a daughter taken to the underworld, a mother searching, and a return linked to the renewal of life.

The Names Eleusis is the sacred place near Athens. Demeter is the goddess who searches for her daughter. Persephone is the daughter taken to the underworld who later returns.

3 · The Myth

Meet Demeter and Persephone first.

Demeter is the mother who searches. Persephone is the daughter who goes below and returns changed. Five moments trace the myth from loss to return.

4 · The Sacred Pattern

The seven movements of the story.

Seven movements offer a modern way of reading the story: invocation, loss, search, descent, revelation, return, and blessing. They are not the recorded order of the ancient secret rites.
